A domicilio

Home pickup & delivery: this easy

We work by appointment, so every garment gets the time it deserves.

Call or message us

Tell us what you need by phone or WhatsApp and we'll find the best time to come and pick up your garment.

We pick up at your door

We come to your home in Can Baró, El Guinardó, El Carmel, Gràcia or anywhere in Barcelona.

We bring it back ready

Your garment comes back altered, checked and ready to wear again.

In Can Baró, pickup and delivery are free. In the rest of Barcelona, just €5 total (pickup + delivery). Hours: Monday to Friday, 10:00–14:00 and 15:00–19:00.

We are Cose Q' Cose

We're a family-run sewing workshop born in the Can Baró neighbourhood, with years of experience in the fashion industry. We love giving garments a second life: we repair, transform and create unique accessories — by hand and from the heart.

Since we went door-to-door, we bring the workshop wherever you need it: your clothes travel, you don't.
